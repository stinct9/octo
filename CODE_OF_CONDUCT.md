# Code of conduct

Octo follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good faith, disagree about the work and never about the person. Harassment,
insults, sharing someone's private information, and sexual or violent language aren't welcome anywhere in the
project: issues, pull requests, discussions, or anywhere you speak for it.

Octo is about conversations, and some of them are about faith, family and work. Never post a recording, a transcript
or anything that identifies someone else in an issue, even your own example.

To report something, write to **support@octolab.online**. Reports are read by the maintainers only and kept private.
The maintainers may remove anything, or anyone, that breaks this code, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines
describe.
