## Unreleased

The first public copy of Octo:

- **The lab** on an Apple Silicon Mac: finds conversations in the recordings, transcribes them (Qwen3-ASR, Whisper for
  the language), tells the speakers apart (pyannote, WeSpeaker voiceprints), reviews each one against your own
  principles with the model you choose, and keeps what to do next. A coach across your reviews, a page per person,
  search, Ask your conversations, Prepare, Talk it through, Rehearse, practice drills, fillers over time, your week,
  the calendar.
- **The Octo app** for the Mac: the lab's pages in a window, the menu-bar octopus, banners, recording calls.
- **The iPhone app**: voice memos, the recorder's Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, the lab's pages away from the Mac.
- **The recorder's firmware** for the Seeed XIAO ESP32S3 Sense on the XIAO expansion board, and its table case.
